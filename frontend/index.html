🚀 Getting Started

Connect your Web3 wallet and select your source and destination tokens. LiquidBridge will automatically find the best route with minimal slippage.

🔄 How Routing Works

Our algorithm analyzes multiple liquidity pools across chains and calculates the optimal swap path to maximize output and minimize price impact.

⛓️ Supported Chains

We support Ethereum, Polygon, Avalanche, BSC, and Arbitrum with instant cross-chain settlement.

💰 Fees

Protocol fee: 0.25% on swaps. No hidden charges. Fee is deducted from output amount.

🔒 Security

Smart contracts are audited and use ReentrancyGuard. All transactions are transparent on-chain.
